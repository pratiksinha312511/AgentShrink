## ── KEEP ON LLM (30) ──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. I worked from 09:00 to 17:00, took a 45-min lunch break, completed 12 tasks, but 3 were revisions. Calculate my net productivity excluding revisions and suggest how to reduce revision rate.
2. Plan a 2-week sprint with 15 stories, 3 developers averaging 4 tasks/day each. Create a day-by-day schedule with buffer time for code reviews.
3. I have 5 team members with different velocities (2, 3, 4, 5, 6 tasks/day). Assign 50 tasks optimally and show the load-balanced schedule.
4. Analyze my work patterns: Mon 6 tasks in 8h, Tue 4 in 7h, Wed 8 in 9h, Thu 3 in 6h, Fri 5 in 7h. Which day am I most productive and why might that be?
5. Calculate the critical path for this project: Task A (3 days, no dependencies), B (2 days, depends on A), C (4 days, no deps), D (1 day, depends on B and C).
6. I have 20 meetings this week averaging 45 minutes each. Calculate what percentage of my 40-hour week is spent in meetings and suggest an optimization strategy.
7. Compare two work strategies: (A) 8 hours straight with declining productivity vs (B) 6 hours in 90-min Pomodoros with breaks. Model productivity curves.
8. Track my productivity over a month with weekly rates: W1: 30 tasks/40h, W2: 35/42h, W3: 25/38h, W4: 40/45h. Identify the trend and predict week 5.
9. I need to complete a 200-hour project. My current velocity is 5.5 hours of focused work per 8-hour day. Calculate realistic finish date accounting for weekends and holidays.
10. Analyze sprint burndown: Day 1: 50 points remaining, Day 2: 47, Day 3: 42, Day 4: 45 (added scope), Day 5: 38. Predict sprint completion probability.
11. Plan resource allocation for 3 concurrent projects with overlapping deadlines and shared team members. Handle conflicts.
12. Calculate the ROI of investing 2 hours per day in automation that saves 15 minutes per task across 100 daily tasks.
13. I tracked my deep work sessions: 2h, 1.5h, 3h, 0.5h, 2.5h across 5 days. Calculate optimal session length for peak productivity.
14. Design a priority matrix for 25 tasks with varying urgency (1-5) and importance (1-5). Use Eisenhower matrix logic to categorize and sequence them.
15. Calculate the cost of context switching: if each switch costs 23 minutes, and I switch 15 times per day, how many productive hours am I losing weekly?
16. Estimate the team's delivery capacity for next quarter: 5 devs, 65 working days, estimated velocity 4 tasks/dev/day, 20% overhead for planning/reviews.
17. Analyze meeting efficiency: 10 meetings this week, 6 had decisions recorded, 4 could have been emails. Calculate the time waste.
18. Plan task batching: 30 tasks across 5 categories. Group them to minimize context switches while meeting deadlines.
19. I need to reduce my 50-hour workweek to 40. Analyze my time breakdown and suggest what to delegate, automate, or eliminate.
20. Calculate the impact of technical debt: we spend 30% of each sprint on maintenance. If we invest 2 sprints in cleanup, maintenance drops to 10%. When does it pay off?
21. Track project health: original estimate 800 hours, 500 hours spent, 60% complete. Predict overshoot and recommend corrective actions.
22. Design a work-from-home productivity framework with hourly goals, break schedules, and accountability checkpoints.
23. Calculate parallel vs sequential task execution: 10 independent tasks at 2h each. How many hours saved with 3 parallel workers vs 1?
24. Analyze sprint velocity trends over 6 sprints: 42, 38, 45, 40, 48, 44 points. Calculate moving average and predict next sprint.
25. Plan a delegation framework: 40 tasks, 4 skill levels required, 5 team members with different skills. Assign optimally.
26. Calculate the true cost of a meeting: 8 attendees × average salary ÷ hourly rate × 1.5 hours + 15 min prep each. Compare with async alternatives.
27. I need to estimate task completion probabilities. Given historical data showing 80% are done on time, what's the probability all 5 tasks in a batch are on time?
28. Design a time-tracking system that captures productive hours, meetings, breaks, and learning time. Calculate weekly efficiency ratios.
29. Analyze the impact of remote work on my team's velocity: office sprint avg 42 points, remote avg 38, hybrid avg 44. What's the optimal arrangement?
30. Create a quarterly capacity plan factoring in holidays, PTO requests, training days, and a 15% buffer for unplanned work.
